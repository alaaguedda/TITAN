    GUIDE

    How to use this dashboard

    1. Globe: press Play epochs to watch detections appear flyby by flyby; click a point for its frame and numbers.
    2. RQ1: drag the min‑area slider — every chart and the correlation matrix re-computes.
    3. RQ2: change the grid; compare counts with the dashed uniform baseline.
    4. RQ3: pick an example, toggle GT/prediction, or use swipe to compare them.
    5. RQ4: move the pixel-shift slider; read the size at which error drops below your tolerance.

    Glossary

    Area physical km² (sum of pixel areas on Titan). deq = 2√(A/π), the diameter of the equal-area circle. Aspect ratio major/minor axis (1 = round). IoU overlap of two masks. Tolerance F1(d) agreement when edges may differ by d pixels.
